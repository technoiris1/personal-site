### yo whats overglade??
so around 50 teenagers from all around the world flew to Singapore for a 4-day long game jam, and it was called Overglade! It was from 29th Jan- 2nd Feb 2026, organised by Hack Club- a global non-profit empowering high-schoolers and encouraging them to code and get into the world of machines.
<br />I was there as an attendee and omg it was amazing- easily, like _easily_ one of the best experiences of my life which I'll cherish forever. The place, the people, the vibe, the memories, the karoake at 3am. Man, it was beautiful.

### planning and preparation
alright so Hack Club is a pretty well-knit community, thanks to the Hack Club Slack which acts as the thread. Though it's slack which is supposed to be all corporate'y, Hack Club Slack is a lot more chill, more of comparable to big discord servers than to big slack workspaces. So around October last year, there was this word in the air that something is coming for Singapore and I was pretty excited for that because it's singapore so cheap flights + easy visa(indian passport lmao ✌️)
<br />Later it was finally announced, A game jam in Singpaore! I and few of my friends in Hack Club decided that yeah we're gonna go. Then came a few months of lockin, we had to ship games in order to qualify and avail travel stipends to cover the flights. I worked a lot, for more than a 100 hours and the day finally came. 

### the day
then the day finally came, I left my house early in the morning for an 8am flight from Jaipur to Mumbai, had a long layover in Mumbai and then boarded my flight to Singapore, it was Singapore Airlines flight 423 at 11:40pm. It's operated on an Airbus A380, holy cow that thing was huge, I had a heard a lot about it earlier and was pretty excited to see it but woah it was huge, the sheer scale I realised after watching the groundsmen standing beside it looking like lego pieces. It's genuinely a high-rise building flying in the air. 
Then it started humming and in a few seconds, it was [airborne](https://airborne.dino.icu/)(pun intended), I ate the inflight meal and everything, the flight was relatively short, around 5.5hrs so I just took a nap and we were about to start our descent by the time i woke up. Then I witnessed some of the most peculiar views of my life, it was a foggy ocean and the whole of it was filled with ships and vessels, and in the backdrop was the mighty singapore skyline.    
![sea](https://cdn.hackclub.com/019dc5d7-3aba-7d1a-913c-99f001a383f4/WhatsApp%20Image%202026-04-25%20at%2023.34.21%20(1).jpeg)

<br />We landed after a while and then it was the good ol' changi airport, way prettier than i expected. It didn't take much time there as the immigration was totally electronic so I just had to scan my passport and that was it. I collected my suitcase and got out. There was one more dude on the same flight as me, he is my good friend. There were people waiting for us at the mac donalds at T2 so we went there and yeah in a while we were out in the city.  

### the big 'pore
this was an inside joke, calling singapore the big 'pore and overglade the 'glade. I was out in the city in like 2hrs of landing, we went to the hotel and put our bags in the storage room as you cannot checkin until like 4pm ig. Then we just had to exist, roam around- sleep deprived with the airlines music banging in your head with a lil bit of schizophrenia. But but but, it was amazing, it was a long long day we spent roaming around the big 'pore. Chinatown, downtown, Marina bay and everything, man that huge mall- me and one more dude were there looking at the shops, the _luxurious_ ones and joking about how you could open a luxury store and name it anything, one was literally deadass named alex's, who tf is alex😭. It was when we realised that its the third time we crossed the same alex's and we had been circling the same big-ass dome for the past hour or so. We certainly needed to be better with directions.
